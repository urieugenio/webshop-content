# market

The best way to experience the collection is in person: see the colours in real light, feel the softness of the merino wool, try on a few shapes and find the one that fits you. At the market I'm always crocheting, so if you visit, you can watch a new piece come to life.

## The Maker Market, De Hallen Amsterdam

- **Saturday 24 October**, 11:00 – 17:00
- **Sunday 25 October**, 12:00 – 17:00

[The Maker Market](https://themakermarket.nl) is a special market full of high-quality, handmade products, and a place to meet new people and exchange ideas with fellow makers. I've been part of it since the very first edition, and it's still one of my favourite places to be.

Pieces sold at the market are one of a kind too. Come early for the best choice.
